How we verify

Each story is checked against independent outlets and, where one exists, the official source. Several outlets agreeing does not make it true: they may be repeating the same agency report.

Where the leaning comes from

The ratings are from AllSides and Media Bias Fact Check, two US organisations. Their left and right scale is designed for the US and does not always fit Spanish or British politics, and they sometimes disagree with each other.

What is a blind spot

A story whose rated sources all sit on the same side, with none in the centre. Unrated sources do not count, so a blind spot does not mean the story is false.

What it does not do

It does not copy articles: the summaries are written here and link to the original. If an official link is missing, the file says so instead of making one up.
